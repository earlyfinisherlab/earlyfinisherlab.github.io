Turn factors and multiples into a mystery your 4th graders will want to finish. Someone took the blue ribbon from the forest fair's prize table - and the only way to find the thief is to do the math.

How It Works

Students help at a forest fair across 7 real-world tasks: a pie contest, sunflower rows, a lantern path, prize tickets, bead patterns, a busy snack stand and a muddy scorecard. Every task ends with a clue question. The answer is a locker number on the Suspect Board, so students cross out one friend at a time. After six tasks two friends are still standing - and the final clue decides which one took the ribbon. Students finish with a written Detective Report.

Self-checking, And No Skipping

  • A wrong answer matches no locker, so students know right away to go back and check their work.
  • Each clue question uses the answers to the other questions on its page ("add your answers to questions 1 and 2"), so students cannot jump straight to the clue.
