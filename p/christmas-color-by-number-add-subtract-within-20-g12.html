Color by number mystery pictures: addition & Subtraction Within 20, 1st and 2nd Grade. Every square on the grid has an addition or subtraction fact. Students solve it, find the answer in the color key, and color the square. When every square is colored, a picture appears.

Christmas-themed from start to finish: all ten mystery pictures are Christmas pictures (a tree, a snowman, a gingerbread man, Santa, a reindeer) and the pages carry tree, present, ornament and gingerbread art.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (18 pages, 10 pictures)

  • Level 1 (Pictures 1-5): addition facts to 20
  • Level 2 (Pictures 6-10): subtraction within 20
  • A color key on every page, with every color named so it works in black and white
  • Answer keys showing each finished picture in color
  • How-to-use page

The pictures: Christmas Tree, Snowman, Gingerbread Man, Ornament, Present, Bell, Santa, Reindeer, Snowflake, Wreath.

Self-checking

Each answer belongs to one color only, so a wrong answer paints a square the wrong color - and it shows in the picture. No two squares that touch have the same fact, so students can't copy the square next door.

Early finishers - math centers - fact fluency practice - homework - sub plans.
